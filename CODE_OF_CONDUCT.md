# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Trade Compass Agent a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and observable behavior, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, private financial data, or another
  person's identifying information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and moderation without harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, sustained
disruption, doxxing, impersonation, and publishing private communications
without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
channels, and other public spaces used to represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Contact the
repository owner through the maintainer profile at
<https://github.com/AdenChenCoder> and request a private reporting channel.
Include links, dates, and a factual description, but do not send secrets or
unrelated private financial data.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates this code. Reports will be reviewed as
privately and impartially as practical.
